# Qavio Support assistant: internal knowledge notes

These notes are what the assistant answers from. Two entries are deliberately out of date compared with
the live help pages, to give the browser checks something real to catch.

- Pricing (article: /help/pricing.html, title "Plans and pricing"): Pro costs $8 per member per month.
- Free plan (article: /help/pricing.html, title "Plans and pricing"): The free plan includes unlimited projects.
- Export (articles: /help/export.html "Exporting your data" and /help/export-guide.html "Export troubleshooting guide"): Tasks can be exported as PDF or CSV.
- Refunds (article: /help/refunds.html, title "Refund policy"): You can get a full refund within 14 days of purchase.
- Password (article: /help/password.html, title "Reset your password"): The reset link is valid for 30 minutes.
- Billing disputes such as a duplicate charge (article: /help/billing.html, title "Billing problems"): Billing disputes, such as a duplicate charge, are handled by a human agent. (The assistant must hand over.)
